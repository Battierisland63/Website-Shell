Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

A Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system helps businesses track client interactions and manage technical support requests in one central place. It ensures customer inquiries are organized, assigned to team members, and resolved in a timely manner.

CRM Comparison

Platform Name Estimated Cost Best For
HubSpot Free to $20/month Startups wanting simple contact tracking and basic ticketing.
Zoho Desk $14 to $40/month Small teams looking for an affordable, full-featured helpdesk.
Freshdesk Free to $15/month Support teams needing clean, intuitive ticket workflows.
