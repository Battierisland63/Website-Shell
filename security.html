Security & Compliance

Protecting company data and customer privacy is an essential responsibility for every team member at Atlantis Corporation. Understanding core security risks and legal standards helps keep our systems safe.

Common Cyber Threats

  • Phishing: Fraudulent emails designed to trick employees into revealing passwords or sensitive information.
  • Ransomware: Malicious software that locks computer files until an extortion payment is made.
  • Weak Passwords: Reused or short passwords that allow attackers to breach accounts using automated guessing.

Key Privacy Laws

Regulation Name One-Line Summary
GDPR European regulation requiring user consent and giving individuals rights over personal data.
CCPA California law allowing residents to see, delete, and control the sale of their personal information.
PCI DSS Global security standards that mandate safe handling and storage of customer payment cards.
