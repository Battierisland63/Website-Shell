Troubleshooting Scenarios

Quick resolution steps for common technical issues encountered by team members at Atlantis Corporation. Select any scenario below to view the recommended fix.

Problem: The power button is pressed, but the machine will not turn on or load the operating system.

Fix:

  1. Check that the power cable is firmly connected to both the computer and the wall outlet.
  2. Confirm the monitor is powered on and the video cable is plugged in properly.
  3. Hold the power button down for 20 seconds to drain residual power, then try turning it on again.

Problem: The workstation cannot load web pages or connect to internal office networks.

Fix:

  1. Ensure the device Wi-Fi switch is turned on or the network cable is clicked in tightly.
  2. Disconnect from the network and reconnect to refresh the computer IP address.
  3. Restart the computer and the local router to clear network configuration errors.

Problem: The user is locked out after entering the wrong password or forgetting their credentials.

Fix:

  1. Verify the employee identity following standard company verification questions.
  2. Confirm Caps Lock is turned off and have the user attempt entry one more time.
  3. Open the administrator portal to unlock the account and set a temporary password.
